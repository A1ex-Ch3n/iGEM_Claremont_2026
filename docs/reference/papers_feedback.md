**"Genomic characterization of the intron-containing T7-like phage phiL7 of *Xanthomonas campestris* pv. *campestris*"**
🔴 Lee, C.N. et al. (2009)

主要是關於phage研究的context和PhiL7作為感染Xcc的phage相關的一些資訊。
- 在當時已經有phage作為農業產品去殺菌了，相似宿主的X. campestris pv. vesicatoria phage就已經被拿來處理tomato bacterial spots, 並且是已經在市場上的產品。
- PhiL7是在台灣被Isolate出來的。
- PhiL7 infect 33 Taiwan isolates of X. campestris pv. campestris. 
- PhiL7 form plaques of 2 to 3 mm in diameter on LB agar plates. 
- PhiL7 require the lipopolysaccharide and the tonB gene cluster of the host for the initial steps of infection.  >> lipopolysaccharide我沒有聽過，但後者的tonB是已經在計劃中的一個重要部分，已經先被拿來跑Boltz-2過了。
- A gene encoding a tail fiber protein with domain duplications, thought to be important for host range determination in X. oryzae pv. oryzae phages, is absent from phiL7.
- We were unable to identify a homolog of the OP1 tail fiber protein (ORF25) thought to be involved in host range determination achieved by variation in the combinations of domain duplications at the N terminus (20).
- 他們也不知道哪個protein is responsible for host range detemination, 所以會拿p20去測試，也就是tail protein III, 他們的測試方法是拿p20 mutant來做做看binding. 
- p20的molecular size是 160 kDa 左右。
